In-town lots and wooded tracts on the edge of Harrison County

Clarksburg has a mix of older in-town lots that have grown up in brush and larger wooded tracts on the edge of Harrison County. This page is for forestry mulching and land clearing in Clarksburg, Nutter Fort, Anmoore, Shinnston, and nearby towns.

In town, the work is often a tight lot, a fence line, or an overgrown hillside behind a house. Outside of town, it is more often hunting land, a driveway through timber, or a farm that needs brush taken back to a usable pasture. Access can be tight on older streets and steep on the hills west of town. Tell the crew how a machine would get in, and whether neighbors, wires, or a narrow lane are part of the picture.

Kingwood and Grafton are farther out in Preston and Taylor Counties. If your land is in one of those towns, pick "Other in North Central WV" on the form and name the town in your message. See also brush clearing, trail clearing, and the Bridgeport page.
