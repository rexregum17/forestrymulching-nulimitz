Homesites and wooded tracts along the I-79 corridor

Bridgeport sits in a growing part of Harrison County, with new homesites along the I-79 corridor and older wooded tracts just a short drive from town. This page is for landowners in Bridgeport and nearby communities who need forestry mulching, lot clearing, or a path opened for a driveway or a building pad.

Homesite work is common here: clearing a wooded lot, leaving selected trees, and getting the ground ready for a builder without stripping every inch of soil. Larger rural parcels still need underbrush cleared, trails opened, or pasture brought back. If you already have a survey or a flagged homesite, mention that in the form. It helps the crew see what should stay and what should be mulched.

Clarksburg is the next town over. If that is a better fit, use the Clarksburg page or pick your town on the form. See also lot clearing and selective clearing.
